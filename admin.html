Assign Time Boost

Give a TIME_BOOST question to a team — if they solve it, they earn extra seconds on their timer.

Assign Boost Question

How Boost Works

1️⃣
Assign the boost
Select the team and a TIME_BOOST question ID. Click Assign.
2️⃣
Team solves it
The team sees it in their Bonus Bidding page and submits their answer to you.
3️⃣
Mark as solved
Go to Review panel and mark it solved. Extra time is automatically added to their countdown.

Create Challenge

Start a head-to-head challenge session between 2–3 teams.

New Challenge Session

Challenge Flow

1️⃣
Start the session
Select a CHALLENGE question and 2–3 teams. Click Start Challenge.
2️⃣
Teams auto-unlock
The Challenge page each team has open will automatically detect the session and display the question.
3️⃣
Pick a winner
Once a team submits, go to Review and mark them as the winner. Points are awarded instantly.

Review & Mark Solved

Mark a team's question submission as solved. Rewards are applied immediately.

Mark as Solved

Reward Reference

What happens when you mark a question solved:

TIME_BOOST
Adds reward_value seconds to the team's extra_time_seconds. Their countdown immediately extends.
CHALLENGE
Marks the challenge session as COMPLETED, sets the winner, and adds reward_value points to their total score.
MAIN
No automatic reward — the main question is scored separately by the admin.
